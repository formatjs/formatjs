# Temporal spec-step audit

Compare observable behavior against [the pinned TC39 text](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/tree/b038883fe9c6aa871ccab3238c90daf9eb5e9b17/spec). Step numbers in this audit refer to that revision, not the moving rendered document. An entry covers the named operation, not its entire chapter or every abstract operation it calls.

| Checked text                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Implementation and boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Temporal and Now object shape and properties](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/blob/b038883fe9c6aa871ccab3238c90daf9eb5e9b17/spec/temporal.html#L4-L99)                                                                                                                                                                             | `index.ts`, `install.ts`, `now.ts`: namespace identity on installation, ordinary objects, eight constructor references, Now methods and tags. A polyfill cannot add engine intrinsics.                                                                                                                      |
| [All six Now methods](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/blob/b038883fe9c6aa871ccab3238c90daf9eb5e9b17/spec/temporal.html#L104-L159) and [clock and local projection](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/blob/b038883fe9c6aa871ccab3238c90daf9eb5e9b17/spec/temporal.html#L166-L244)                                            | `now.ts` and `crates/temporal/now.rs`: zone before clock, single sample, bounds and result type. | [Date.prototype.toTemporalInstant](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/blob/b038883fe9c6aa871ccab3238c90daf9eb5e9b17/spec/mainadditions.html#L788-L795)                                                                                                                                                                                 | `date-bridge.ts`: native `getTime` performs the receiver check and slot read, `BigInt` rejects an invalid date, and scaling occurs before allocation.                                                                                                                                                       |

This does not certify the complete instant-string grammar, time-zone conversion, formatting algorithms or cross-realm behavior.        |

A missing field in `with` preserves the old value; a supplied zero replaces it.                               |

## Not yet checked step by step

- Constructors, allocation and cross-realm prototype selection; parsing, formatting, coercion and option-reading helpers.
- Duration arithmetic, rounding and differences; the remaining Instant operations.
- PlainDate, PlainDateTime, PlainTime, PlainYearMonth, PlainMonthDay and ZonedDateTime operations other than the narrow entries above.
- Calendar calculations, zone transition and disambiguation algorithms, shared abstract operations, and ECMA-402 / Date integration outside the Date bridge.

Test262, Hermes and ICU checks provide independent evidence. A passing suite does not change an unchecked item into a reviewed one.
